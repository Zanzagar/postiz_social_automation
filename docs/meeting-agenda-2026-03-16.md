# Gita Valley Social Automation — Meeting Agenda

## 1. The Problem (1-2 min)
"We manage 5+ social platforms — Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, LinkedIn. Each needs different formatting, hashtags, tone. It's all manual, takes hours, and posts are inconsistent. This tool solves that."

## 2. Live Demo (10 min)

### Create a Post (the wow moment)
- Type a real post (e.g., Sunday feast announcement)
- Select platforms: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn
- Pick a date
- Hit Generate — AI creates tailored captions for each platform
- Point out: IG gets hashtags, LinkedIn gets professional tone, Facebook gets community warmth

### Calendar
- Posts grouped by date with status badges (draft/scheduled/posted)
- "Our entire content pipeline at a glance"

### AI Suggestions
- AI-generated content ideas with pillars and rationale
- Follows our 40/25/15/10/5/5 content pillar mix

### Health Dashboard
- All services green — Sheets, Postiz, Claude AI, OAuth

## 3. Platform Connection Discussion (5 min)

**The situation:** To actually publish posts, we need to connect our social accounts through Postiz. Each platform requires a "developer app" registration.

**Easy platforms (days to set up):**
- X/Twitter
- LinkedIn
- TikTok

**Harder platforms (but we have a shortcut):**
- Facebook, Instagram, Threads require Meta developer app approval
- Full approval can take 1+ month with privacy policy, terms of service, business verification
- **Our shortcut: Meta Development Mode** — no approval needed, just add our accounts as test users
- Works perfectly for a single organization posting to its own accounts
- Privacy policy already exists: gitavalley.org/privacy-policy/

**What we need from you:**
- Meta Business Suite admin access (whoever manages the Facebook page)
- Which platforms are highest priority?
- Do we have a Google Business Profile to connect?
